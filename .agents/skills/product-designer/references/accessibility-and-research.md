# Accessibility and Research

Research must distinguish hypotheses from validated interviews; cite public evidence and obtain consent for interviews. Document geographic and language sampling gaps.

Check keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic headings/landmarks, explicit labels, field errors, contrast, 200% zoom, 320px reflow, accessible slider name/value, chart text alternatives, reduced motion, mobile touch targets and low-bandwidth resilience.

Design explicit creator rights, category correction, edit/withdraw and reporting pathways before public publishing. Record each finding with date, environment, steps, expected/actual result, severity and evidence.
