# Hackathon recording runbook (NW-07)

## Evidence baseline

- Live site: https://funky-amahle.github.io/newwave-africa/
- Source: https://github.com/Funky-Amahle/newwave-africa
- Record the exact main SHA and completed Pages deployment run before filming.
- Eight starter stories are **fictional samples**; written creator drafts stay in **browser localStorage** and are not publicly published.
- Category suggestions use **local keyword rules**, not a live AI classifier. Diversity ranking and dashboard are prototype illustrations, not validated fairness claims.

## Preflight (human recording-browser QA)

1. Wait for the matching main SHA Pages workflow to finish successfully; hard-refresh the live site.
2. At 375 px and desktop width, inspect search/category inputs, story-card Listen and voice selector alignment, footer wrapping, focus states and contrast.
3. Press Listen, Pause, Resume, Stop, change voices, and **confirm audible sound** with headphones. Browser voices vary by device.
4. Grant microphone permission in a secure browser context; record 10 seconds; confirm waveform activity, playback and download; discard the test recording.
5. Test keyboard-only navigation, creator rights confirmation, draft preview and local save; avoid private or copyrighted test material.
6. Disable notifications; use sample stories and a fictional test draft; capture a backup screen recording.

Do not mark audio or microphone tests passed based only on a build, source inspection or CI.

## Export and review

- Capture at 1920×1080, 30 fps; export H.264 MP4 with AAC audio, under 500 MB.
- Aim for ~5 minutes each. Add readable captions, clear pointer movements and a quiet voiceover.
- Verify captions against spoken text, remove personal data, confirm rights to all media and check playback of the exported MP4.
- Submit only after a human has watched both exported videos end-to-end.

## Approval boundary

This runbook does not authorize merge, deployment, publishing or recording on the user's device. Obtain explicit human approval before merge or deployment.
