# External Publication Checklist

- [ ] Claims are supported by current evidence and independent review.
- [ ] Sources, authors, dates and attribution are accurate.
- [ ] License or permission covers the intended use; unresolved rights escalated.
- [ ] No sensitive or private information is disclosed.
- [ ] Cultural and linguistic framing reviewed where relevant.
- [ ] Human approver, date, version and channel recorded.

Never auto-publish from a verification status alone.
